#4471 holds two problems, not one

owner lstolcman proposed reviewed 2026-11-11 · 0 days ago

The incident page for #4471 says it is one issue with one fix queued. The 412 reports merged into it do not agree. Built at render time from the export and the desk’s own grouping, they fall into two causes, and only one of them is the parser.

The split

5 of these 379 read as a one-day move. They stay counted under parser, filed by tenant, as the desk does.

Why the desk saw one problem

Triage merges on the subject line alone (decision 0001). People describe a wrong day in the same few words whatever the cause, so the reports merged into one ticket. The subject says “wrong day”; the tenant and the way in say which bug.

What each ticket asks for

  • parser (379 reports): read dates in the tenant’s order. Every tenant on file writes day-first, and the shared parser reads month-first.
  • hint (33 reports): booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY to day-first tenants, so their people type the month in the day field.

Both tickets are filed in the desk’s tracker, one per cause, each with its count.

What is not a third problem

Five reports describe a booking that moved by one day, not a month flip. desk-notes.json lists them, the component footnotes them, and they stay counted under parser where the tenant files them. They are the same code path, read differently.

How the desk closed them

The desk closed #4471 on 5 October. Its engineering update marks parser FIXED (fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales) and hint WON’T FIX, because booking-v3 is retired. Both tickets in the tracker are closed the same way: #1 completed, #2 not planned.
